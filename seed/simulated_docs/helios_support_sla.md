# Helios Platform — Enterprise Support Service Level Agreement (Schedule C)

## C.1 Severity definitions
Severity 1 is a total loss of production service or a confirmed data integrity
incident. Severity 2 is a major function unavailable with no workaround.
Severity 3 is a degraded function with an available workaround.

## C.2 Response and restoration targets
Severity 1: first response within 30 minutes, target restoration within 4
hours.
Severity 2: first response within 2 hours, target restoration within 1 business
day.
Severity 3: first response within 1 business day.

## C.3 Coverage window
Severity 1 support is provided 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including public
holidays. Severity 2 and Severity 3 are covered Monday to Friday, 08:00 to
20:00 IST, excluding public holidays.

## C.4 Service credits
Where Helios fails to meet the Severity 1 restoration target, the customer is
entitled to a service credit of 5 percent of the monthly platform fee for each
complete hour of delay. Service credits are capped at 20 percent of the monthly
platform fee in any single calendar month, and at 50 percent in any contract
year.

## C.5 Claiming credits
Service credits are not automatic. The customer must submit a claim within 30
days of the incident, referencing the incident ticket number. Credits are
applied against the next invoice and are not payable in cash.

## C.6 Exclusions
The targets in C.2 do not apply during scheduled maintenance windows notified
at least 5 business days in advance, or to incidents caused by customer
modifications to the platform.
