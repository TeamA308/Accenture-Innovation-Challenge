# Northwind Retail — Manufacturer Warranty Terms (consumer electronics)

## W.1 Duration
The standard manufacturer warranty covers 24 months from the date of invoice.
Extended cover of a further 12 months may be purchased at the point of sale.

## W.2 What is covered
The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. It is
transferable if the product is resold together with the original invoice.

## W.3 What is not covered
Physical damage, water ingress, unauthorised repair, cosmetic wear, and
consumable parts such as batteries after the first 6 months are excluded.

## W.4 Support availability
Warranty support is available Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm IST, through the
Northwind service desk and authorised service partners.

## W.5 Turnaround
In-warranty repairs are completed within 10 business days of the unit reaching
an authorised service centre. Where a repair is not economical, a replacement
of equivalent specification is offered.
