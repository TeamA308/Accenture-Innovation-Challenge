# Northwind Retail — Returns and Refunds Policy (v4.2, effective 1 January 2026)

## 1. Scope
This policy governs all consumer purchases made through Northwind Retail online
and in-store channels in India. It does not apply to business-to-business
supply agreements, which are governed by the relevant master services
agreement.

## 2. Standard refund window
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery. The 30 day window is
measured from the date the carrier records delivery, not the date of order.
Requests received after 30 days are declined unless a statutory right applies.

## 3. Exclusions
The following categories are final sale and carry no refund entitlement:
clearance items, personalised or engraved items, opened consumables, and
digital gift cards. Priority tier membership does not extend the refund window;
it only shortens the processing time.

## 4. Return shipment
A refund is issued only after the item is received back at a Northwind
warehouse and passes inspection. There is no value threshold below which the
return shipment is waived. Northwind pays return shipping only where the item
was faulty or incorrectly supplied.

## 5. Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 business
days of inspection. Priority tier members are processed within 3 business days.

## 6. Partial refunds
Where an item shows use beyond reasonable inspection, a partial refund of up to
70 percent of the purchase price may be offered at the branch manager's
discretion.
